Before You Begin

IntegrityFlow Monitoring & Privacy Notice (Section 10.4)

Please review the automated monitoring guidelines below. We believe in transparent, privacy-first proctoring so you know exactly what is and is not monitored during your examination.
👁️ What is Monitored During Your Exam
👤

Camera & Visual Orientation

In online exams, monitors head orientation (sustained looking away) and checks for multiple faces or unauthorized objects (such as mobile devices) in view. Camera and voice checks are skipped in physical-lab mode.

🖥️

Screen & Application Environment

Enforces the exam process whitelist to ensure prohibited background applications and communication tools are closed.

🎙️

Microphone & Speaker Verification (Online Exams)

During self-check, you record an eight-second voice sample and a separate four-second confirmation. The application stores a session-specific speaker reference vector on this computer. This is biometric voice data used to compare exam speech with your enrolled voice.

During the exam, microphone audio is processed locally in short segments. A possible unfamiliar-speaker alert can save a short WAV recording on this computer and upload it as evidence to the exam server or its configured storage service. Your examiner and authorized administrators can review it. A voice mismatch is a signal for human review, not proof of misconduct.

🔒 Our Privacy & Data Protection Commitments
🚫

Continuous Video is NEVER Recorded or Stored

No continuous video or full-exam audio recording is saved or uploaded. Camera frames and microphone segments are processed locally. Identity photos, violation stills and suspicious audio clips described in this notice may be retained.

📸

Identity Photo & Event Evidence

Online self-check may save an identity photo. Potential violations may save a screen screenshot or annotated camera still, and voice alerts may include a short audio clip, for examiner review.

🛡️

No Facial Recognition Database

The system does not identify you using a facial recognition database. The session-specific speaker reference vector described above is stored for voice comparison.

🗂️

Retention & Deletion

This version has no automatic expiry for voice reference vectors or saved audio evidence. They remain on this computer and, for uploaded clips, in the server's configured storage until an authorized operator manually removes them. Closing the application or submitting the exam does not delete them.

Ask your examiner or exam coordinator about the institution's retention period and how to request removal of local and uploaded copies. Raw enrollment recordings are processed for calibration and are not saved as audio files by this application.

Decline Exam Monitoring?

Declining consent means you cannot proceed with this online examination on IntegrityFlow. The application will close and you will need to contact your instructor or exam coordinator.
